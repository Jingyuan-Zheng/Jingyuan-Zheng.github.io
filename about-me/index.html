Research & Analytical Projects

Reliability Modelling and Decision Support for PV Microinverter Systems

Nov 2024 - Jul 2025

MSc Dissertation · The University of Hong Kong · Supervisor: Prof. Yao Cheng

  • Developed a reliability-driven decision-support model for PV microinverter spare parts, linking stochastic failure behaviour, replenishment lead time, downtime loss, lifecycle cost, and carbon-emission impact.
  • Applied CTMC-based reliability modelling, NSGA-II multi-objective optimisation, and Monte Carlo simulation to evaluate inventory policies under uncertain failures and supply conditions.
  • Conducted sensitivity analysis and translated model outputs into practical recommendations for inventory levels, lead-time control, reliability improvement, and system resilience.

System Dynamics Simulation of Operational Risk Evolution in Maritime Supply Chains

Oct 2023 - Jun 2024

Undergraduate Dissertation · Fuzhou University · Supervisor: Prof. Haidong Yang

  • Built a system dynamics model to simulate operational risk evolution in an international maritime supply-chain system using a three-tier risk indicator framework and questionnaire-based data.
  • Applied questionnaire reliability testing and factor analysis to process survey responses, determine indicator weights, and convert qualitative risk factors into model inputs.
  • Generated scenario-based recommendations for supplier reliability, logistics coordination, information-flow control, financial health, and operational resilience.

Machine Learning Classification of High-Dimensional Time-Series Signals

Dec 2024

Academic Project · The University of Hong Kong · Directed by Dr. Shujia Qin

  • Developed Python-based training and inference workflows for binary classification of 8,192-point time-series signals under limited domain information and a small labelled dataset.
  • Extracted and compared time-domain, frequency-domain, and power spectral density features to guide feature engineering and model design.
  • Implemented and compared a stacking ensemble, an RBF-kernel SVM, and a neural-network model with Bayesian hyperparameter tuning, documenting performance, limitations, and reproducibility.
